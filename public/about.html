About the stylists at Yuzu Hair & Beauty

Yuzu Hair & Beauty is a Japanese hair salon London guests find in Dickens Yard, Ealing. The team’s work is Japanese-inspired precision: clean cuts, considered colour, and finishes that last beyond the chair. Guests describe the salon as calm, expert and focused on detail. One Google review calls Jasmine “a brilliant artist working in hair”.

The salon sits at 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD. It is a short walk from Ealing Broadway station, so a hairdresser Ealing Broadway search should land on this address, not the old High Street or Mattock Lane listings.

Hours are Tuesday to Friday 10am–8pm and Saturday 9am–6pm. Phone 020 8840 2244. Book via Phorest. Colour appointments need a patch test. Offers include Colour Tuesdays (50% off the most expensive colour with a senior stylist plus a full-priced wash, cut and blow-dry), Smooth Wednesdays (25% off Aura smoothing), and Thursday colour with a stylist.

Follow the team on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok for in-salon colour — blonde layers, copper balayage, and weekday offers. Join-the-team roles are listed on the current site when vacancies are open.
