Questions about Yuzu Hair & Beauty in Ealing

Straight answers from the Japanese-inspired hair salon at 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD. Phone 020 8840 2244. These are the same facts as on the price list and the visit block — written so answer engines can quote them.

How much is a haircut at Yuzu in Ealing?

A ladies wash, cut and style starts at £58 with a stylist and £87 with a senior stylist. Gents wash, cut and style is £58 with a senior stylist. Kids 12 and under start at £29. Teens 13–16 from £35. Blow-dries start at £41. Colour, balayage and treatments are on the HTML price list.

Do I need a patch test before hair colour?

Yes. Colour services need a mandatory patch test for every client, including existing guests, at least 48 hours before the appointment. Book the test on Phorest. Read the 2025 patch-testing notes. We do not take walk-in colour without a patch test.

Which hair salons in Ealing are near the station?

Yuzu Hair & Beauty is in Dickens Yard on Longfield Avenue, about a two-minute walk from Ealing Broadway station. It is a Japanese hair salon London visitors use in west London for cuts, colour, balayage and Brazilian blow-dry — not the Nottingham business of a similar name.

What are the opening hours?

Tuesday to Friday 10am–8pm, Saturday 9am–6pm. Monday and Sunday the salon is closed.

How do I book?

Book on Phorest or the Phorest salon profile, or call 020 8840 2244. Say whether you want a senior stylist or stylist so the price matches the chair. Email info@yuzuhairandbeauty.co.uk.
