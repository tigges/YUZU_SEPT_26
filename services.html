Hair salon services in Ealing Broadway

Yuzu Hair & Beauty is a Japanese-inspired hair salon at 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, London W5 2TD. Stylists work on precision cuts, colour, balayage, foiliage, treatments and Aura smoothing (Brazilian blow-dry), formaldehyde and ammonia free.

Cut & styling

Ladies wash, cut and style from £58 with a stylist and £87 with a senior stylist. Gents wash, cut and style is £58 with a senior stylist. Kids 12 and under from £29. Teens 13–16 from £35. Blow-dries from £41. Cuts are tailored to hair, face shape, and how you actually live.

Colour

Roots from £75, full head from £87, Illumina colouring from £133 with a senior stylist. In-between colour services sit above those prices. Every colour guest, including existing clients, needs a mandatory patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment.

Highlights, balayage and foiliage

T-section from £64, half head from £75, full head from £98 with a stylist. Long hair below the chest adds £41. Toner is £38. Blending is £41. These are the services people search as balayage Ealing and hair colour Ealing.

Treatments

Nashi Filler Express £33; Nashi Filler 1, 2, 3 intense moisture £46. K2.0 moisture from £41 short to £75 extra long. Aura smoothing from £117 short to £357 long, lasting about 3–4 months. Smooth Wednesdays take 25% off Aura smoothing.
